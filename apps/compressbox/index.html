Frequently asked questions

Where are compressed files saved?

Completed files are saved locally to your Downloads folder.

Why didn’t my file get much smaller?

Already optimized files may not become much smaller without quality loss. If output size grows, try a lower quality setting or retain the original format.

Does it change video size or frame rate?

Videos keep their original dimensions and frame rate by default. You can adjust the compression quality for each video before starting.

What do I need to run it?

macOS 13 or later.
