CompressBox Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

Our privacy commitment

CompressBox processes images and videos entirely on your Mac. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal data, image data, video data, or usage data with the developer or third parties.

Files and processing

Files added to CompressBox are read and compressed locally on your device. The app does not upload files to a server. Compressed output is saved locally to your Downloads folder.

Data collection and tracking

CompressBox has no accounts, analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs, cloud syncing, or developer-operated backend service. It does not collect identifiers, contact information, diagnostics, or usage information.

Network access

CompressBox does not use network services to process your files. It does not send your files or personal information over the network.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes these data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before that version is released.

Support

For support, visit the CompressBox Support page or email support@xingjii.com.
