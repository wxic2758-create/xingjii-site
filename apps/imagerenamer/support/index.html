Rename images in seconds

ImageRenamer is a private, offline batch-renaming tool for macOS. Add images or folders, preview the result, then export renamed copies.

Quick tips

  • Use Add Images or Add Folder to import files.
  • Choose a prefix, suffix, start number, and digit count.
  • Review the live filename preview before exporting.
  • Select an output folder when macOS asks for permission.

Get support

Questions, bug reports, or feedback are welcome. Please include your macOS version and a short description of what happened. Do not attach private images unless they are necessary to reproduce the issue.

Need smaller image files?

CompressBox compresses images and videos locally on your Mac.

Reporting a bug?

Please include your macOS version, the app version, and a short description of what happened. Do not include passwords, private keys, or private images unless they are needed to reproduce the issue.
